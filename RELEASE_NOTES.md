# AlexLMP Revived 2.3

## New look: Stream
A clean, familiar streaming-app style that anyone can pick up, now the default.
- Home: greeting, quick chips (YouTube Music, On this phone, AI DJ, Ask AI), a grid of quick tiles for your most-played stuff, then rows of covers
- Search: a big search bar and colorful Browse all tiles
- Your Library: one list of playlists, albums and artists with filter chips, Recents or A to Z sort, and a list/grid switch. Liked Songs and Downloaded stay pinned at the top

- New Stream player: sits on a color wash from the cover, big art that eases back when paused, heart next to the title, white play button, shuffle and repeat with dots when on, plus lyrics, sound tools, sleep timer and queue
- Dark by default, light theme in Settings > Look. Play Music and Expressive layouts are still there under Layout

AlexLMP Revived 2.1 brings the new look to every screen.

## New
- **Albums, singles, playlists and Liked songs** are rebuilt in the new style: big shaped cover, Play / Shuffle buttons, save, download and more. Albums show the exact release date and more from the artist. Playlists and Liked songs have a search box and sorting.
- **New artist pages** with a big photo, top songs (with Show all), albums, singles and EPs, related artists and an About section. "See all" lists everything they've released.
- **4 Home styles:** Expressive, Compact, Magazine and Grid. Pick one in You > Look or in Settings.
- **Tab bar options:** Docked, Floating or Icons only. Add a Stats tab or an AI tab, hide the Search tab, and choose which tab the app opens on.
- **New song menu** with Play next, Add to queue, Like, Add to playlist, Download, Go to album/artist and Song info.
- **New Settings page** with everything in one place.
- **Speed & pitch:** a Back to normal button, Sped Up, Slowed Down, Nightcore and Slowed & Reverb presets, a switch to sync speed and pitch, and a reverb slider.
- **EQ reset button.**

## Fixed
- The heart on album pages now saves the album to your Library (it used to like only the first song).
- Pitch changes right away instead of waiting for the next song.
- Every button squishes on a quick tap, not just when you hold it.
- The equalizer moved to the bottom of Sound & tools so you don't drag it by accident while scrolling.
- Search no longer shows the Songs / Albums / Artists filters before you've typed anything.

## From 2.0
## New look
- **Colors from your music.** The whole app takes its colors from the song that's playing and fades smoothly to the next song's colors. You can turn this off and pick a color in You > Look.
- **Squishy, springy everything.** Buttons, cards and tabs squish when you press them and bounce back. Shapes morph, lists pop in, and screens glide between tabs.
- **Bold shapes.** Album art, avatars and cards come in cookie, clover, flower and burst shapes.
- **New Home.** A carousel of what you've been playing, a Shuffle / Liked / AI DJ button row, a "This week" stats card and all your mixes.
- **New Search.** A big search bar with voice search, filters for songs, artists, albums, playlists and videos, your recent searches, and colorful genres to browse.
- **New Library.** Liked and Downloads tiles up top, plus playlists, artists, albums, songs and downloads with list or grid view and A–Z sorting.
- **New You tab.** Your account, your stats and all your settings in one place.
- **Floating mini player** with a wavy progress line. Swipe it sideways to skip, or up to open the player.

## Player
- **7 player styles:** Expressive, Canvas, Vinyl, Tonal card, Bloom, Lyrics first and Minimal. Switch from the player's menu or in You > Player style.
- **Tap the album art** to switch between the cookie shape and a normal rounded square. It morphs between them and remembers your choice.
- **Swipe the art** left or right to change songs, double-tap it to like the song, and drag the player down to close it.
- **Wavy seek bar** that wiggles while music plays and flattens when you pause.
- **Big connected Previous / Play / Next buttons.** The one you press grows, and the play button changes shape when you play or pause.

## Features
- **Live synced lyrics.** Full-screen lyrics that follow along, with the current line big and glowing. Tap any line to jump to it.
- **Sound & tools.** A real equalizer with presets (Bass boost, Vocal, Treble, Loud), crossfade, sleep timer, speed, pitch and skip silence, all in one sheet.
- **Listening stats.** Minutes listened, your streak, your peak hour, a listening-by-hour chart, and your top artists and songs for the week, month or all time.
- **Queue.** See what's playing now, what's up next and what comes later. Swipe to remove songs.

Updating installs over your current version. Your library, likes and settings stay.
