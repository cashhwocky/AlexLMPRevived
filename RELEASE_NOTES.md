All your music, one player.

AlexLMP Revived 2.0 is a complete redesign in Material 3 Expressive.

## New look
- **Colors from your music.** The whole app takes its colors from the song that's playing and fades smoothly to the next song's colors. You can turn this off and pick a color in You > Look.
- **Squishy, springy everything.** Buttons, cards and tabs squish when you press them and bounce back. Shapes morph, lists pop in, and screens glide between tabs.
- **Bold shapes.** Album art, avatars and cards come in cookie, clover, flower and burst shapes.
- **New Home.** A carousel of what you've been playing, a Shuffle / Liked / AI DJ button row, a "This week" stats card and all your mixes.
- **New Search.** A big search bar with voice search, filters for songs, artists, albums, playlists and videos, your recent searches, and colorful genres to browse.
- **New Library.** Liked and Downloads tiles up top, plus playlists, artists, albums, songs and downloads with list or grid view and A–Z sorting.
- **New You tab.** Your account, your stats and all your settings in one place.
- **Floating mini player** with a wavy progress line. Swipe it sideways to skip, or up to open the player.

## Player
- **7 player styles:** Expressive, Canvas, Vinyl, Tonal card, Bloom, Lyrics first and Minimal. Switch from the player's menu or in You > Player style.
- **Tap the album art** to switch between the cookie shape and a normal rounded square. It morphs between them and remembers your choice.
- **Swipe the art** left or right to change songs, double-tap it to like the song, and drag the player down to close it.
- **Wavy seek bar** that wiggles while music plays and flattens when you pause.
- **Big connected Previous / Play / Next buttons.** The one you press grows, and the play button changes shape when you play or pause.

## Features
- **Live synced lyrics.** Full-screen lyrics that follow along, with the current line big and glowing. Tap any line to jump to it.
- **Sound & tools.** A real equalizer with presets (Bass boost, Vocal, Treble, Loud), crossfade, sleep timer, speed, pitch and skip silence, all in one sheet.
- **Listening stats.** Minutes listened, your streak, your peak hour, a listening-by-hour chart, and your top artists and songs for the week, month or all time.
- **Queue.** See what's playing now, what's up next and what comes later. Swipe to remove songs.

Updating installs over your current version. Your library, likes and settings stay.

If you enjoy AlexLMP Revived, you can support the project from the card at the top of Settings <3
